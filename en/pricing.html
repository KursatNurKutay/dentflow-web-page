DentFlow Pricing

DentFlow works on a modular pricing model. You start with DentFlow Basic, add the communication channels your clinic needs, and, if you'd like, choose voice replies separately for each channel.

STEP 1

DentFlow Basic

The base subscription that runs on your website, knows your clinic, and handles 24/7 written communication with your patients.

STEP 2

Add Channels

Add whichever of WhatsApp, Instagram and Messenger/Facebook fit your clinic — one at a time or all together.

STEP 3

Choose Voice

Turn on voice replies separately for each channel — text where you want it, voice where you want it.

STEP 4

Setup & Payment

You'll find the one-time setup fee and the subscription billing schedule below.

Setup Fee and Payment Terms

DentFlow has a one-time setup and configuration fee of €300.

Stage 1 · At Signing
€150

Non-refundable
Stage 2 · Ready for Use
€150 + first 6 months

Once DentFlow has been fully configured and is ready for use, the remaining €150 setup fee is collected together with the first 6 months of the selected subscription.

After That · Every 6 Months
6-Month Period

Subscriptions are billed in 6-month periods and collected at the start of each new period.

Total setup fee: €300 (€150 at signing + €150 when ready for use)
