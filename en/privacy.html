Privacy Policy (KVKK Notice)

Last updated: September 3, 2026

1. Data Controller

This notice is prepared by DentFlow (a brand operating under KOBY SOFT, Girne, Northern Cyprus) in its capacity as data controller, in accordance with Turkey's Law No. 6698 on the Protection of Personal Data ("KVKK").

2. Scope — The Difference Between This Site and the DentFlow Assistant

This notice covers only the data collected by this marketing website (dentflowclinic.com) — that is, the data you share when you visit this site or fill out the contact form. Conversations you have as a patient with the DentFlow assistant through a dental clinic's WhatsApp, Instagram, Messenger or website are processed under that clinic's own privacy policy and under DentFlow's separate data processing agreements with its clinic customers, with the additional care required for health data. For questions about conversations you had with DentFlow as a patient at a clinic, please contact that clinic directly.

3. What Personal Data We Collect

When you contact us through our website, we may process the following personal data:

  • First and last name
  • Email address
  • Phone number (if you provide one)
  • The content of the message you send through the contact form

4. How Personal Data Is Collected and Its Legal Basis

Your personal data is collected directly from you electronically when you fill out the contact form on our website or contact us by email. This data is processed on the legal grounds of "explicit consent of the data subject" and "being directly related to the establishment or performance of a contract," as set out in Article 5 of KVKK.

5. Purposes of Processing Personal Data

The personal data we collect is processed solely for the following purposes:

  • Responding to your request or question
  • Providing information about DentFlow's products and services
  • Maintaining and keeping a record of our communication with you

6. Transfer of Personal Data

Your personal data is not shared with third parties or sold for marketing purposes, except where required by law. Your data may only be shared with infrastructure providers who help us deliver our services (for example, hosting and email service providers), to the extent the service requires.

7. Retention Period

Your personal data is retained for as long as required by the purpose of processing or by the applicable statutory limitation periods; at the end of these periods it is deleted, destroyed, or anonymized.

8. Your Rights Under KVKK Article 11

Under Article 11 of KVKK, you may apply to us to: learn whether your personal data is being processed; request information about it if it has been processed; learn the purpose of its processing and whether it is used in accordance with that purpose; know the third parties, domestic or abroad, to whom it has been transferred; request correction if it has been processed incompletely or incorrectly; request its deletion or destruction within the conditions set out in KVKK; object to a result that arises against you from the analysis of your processed data exclusively through automated systems; and request compensation for any damage you suffer due to unlawful processing.

9. How to Apply

To exercise the rights listed above, you can send your requests by email to info@kobysoft.app. Your request will be concluded free of charge, as soon as possible and, at the latest, within the legally required period, depending on its nature.

10. Cookies

Our website may use cookies or similar analytics tools to improve the visitor experience and analyze site usage. This section will be updated with the necessary information once such tools are put into use.

11. Changes

This notice may be revised from time to time in line with changes in legal regulations or updates to our business processes. The current version is always published on this page.
