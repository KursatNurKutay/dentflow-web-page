What is DentFlow Assistant?

DentFlow Assistant is a 24/7 digital patient communication assistant built for dental clinics.

It integrates easily into your website, learns everything about your clinic, automatically detects your patients' language, and helps them 24/7.

It can handle multiple patients at the same time.

If you'd like, it also connects to WhatsApp, Instagram and Messenger. It answers your patients' questions and streamlines every part of the front-desk process, lightening your staff's communication workload.
