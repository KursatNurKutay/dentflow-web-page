KOBY SOFT

Smart solutions for growing businesses.

KOBY SOFT's journey began in 1984, with one of the first personal computers to enter our homes: the Commodore 64.

In the 42 years since, we've watched computers move from our homes to the center of business life; we lived through the internet revolution, watched the world go mobile, embraced e-commerce wholeheartedly, saw cloud technology erase borders, and now we're learning to live, work and create with Artificial Intelligence.

KOBY SOFT is the result of that entire technology journey.

Technology changed, the tools changed, the way we work changed. But one thing never did: the curiosity to not just follow new technology, but to turn it into something that actually works in real life.

That's exactly what we do today with KOBY SOFT.

We turn complex technologies into solutions that are simple, usable, and genuinely beneficial for businesses. We see AI not as a showpiece, but as a tool that works in the everyday life of a business.

For us, it's not about "AI" — it's about what it can actually do for businesses.

It's about making technology a natural part of the business.
